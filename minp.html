Defence Services Institute of Nursing and Paramedical Science

The Defence Services Nursing and Paramedical Academy (MINP), is one of military-run institutes of higher learning in Myanmar.The university offers bachelor's, master's and ph.D degree programs in Nursing, a bachelor's degree program in Pharmacy, a bachelor's degree program in Paramedical Science.
