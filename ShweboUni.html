Shwebo University

Shwebo also known as the rice barn of Upper Myanmar, is not far from the ancient Pyu Palace of Han Lin, the world heritage site of UNESCO. It is in the Sagaing Region, in central Myanmar.

Official Site:

sbu.edu.mm

📍 Campus Location: [Shwebo, Sagaing]

Offered Degrees (ဘွဲ့များ)

  • Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) - သိပ္ပံဘွဲ့4 Years
  • Bachelor of Law (LL.B.) - ဥပဒေဘွဲ့5 Years
