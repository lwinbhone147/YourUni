Explore Career Paths

🏗️
🔥 High Demand

Civil Engineering

What you'll do

Plan, design, and supervise the construction of essential public infrastructure, including highways, bridges, water systems, and large buildings.

Where you'll work

Major construction firms (e.g., Shwe Taung, Max Myanmar), urban planning departments, and civil engineering consultancies.

🔌
⚡ Hardware

Electronic Engineering

What you'll do

Design and develop electronic circuits, microprocessors, and embedded systems for consumer electronics, medical devices, and control systems.

Where you'll work

Semiconductor companies, consumer electronics manufacturers, telecommunications firms, and embedded systems R&D labs.

⚡
💡 Essential

Electrical Power Engineering

What you'll do

Generate, transmit, and distribute electrical power. Design high-voltage grids, renewable energy systems, and industrial electrical networks.

Where you'll work

Power distribution boards (e.g., YESC, EPC), renewable energy corporations, and industrial manufacturing plants.

⚙️
🛠️ Core Role

Mechanical Engineering

What you'll do

Apply physics and materials science to design, analyze, and manufacture mechanical systems, engines, HVAC units, and heavy machinery.

Where you'll work

Automotive assembly lines, industrial manufacturing facilities, energy sector plants, and heavy equipment engineering firms.

📡
📶 Connectivity

Telecommunication Engineering

What you'll do

Design and manage communication networks, including fiber optics, satellite transmission, 5G cellular towers, and broad-scale internet infrastructure.

Where you'll work

Telecom operators (ATOM, MPT, Ooredoo), internet service providers, and network infrastructure firms.

💻
🔥 High Demand

Computer Engineering & IT

What you'll do

Bridge hardware and software by designing computing architecture, developing enterprise software systems, and managing robust IT networks.

Where you'll work

Tech startups, corporate IT departments, fintech platforms, software development agencies, and data centers.

🤖
🚀 Future Tech

Mechatronic Engineering

What you'll do

Combine mechanical, electronic, and software engineering to develop automated manufacturing lines, smart robotics, and autonomous systems.

Where you'll work

Advanced manufacturing plants, robotics startups, aerospace tech firms, and industrial automation R&D labs.

🧪
🏭 Industrial

Chemical Engineering

What you'll do

Apply chemistry and engineering principles to design processes for producing fuels, pharmaceuticals, food products, and advanced materials.

Where you'll work

Oil and gas corporations, pharmaceutical manufacturers, environmental agencies, and chemical processing plants.

🥫
🌾 FMCG

Food Engineering

What you'll do

Design industrial processes for food production, focusing on preservation, packaging, quality control, and large-scale nutritional extraction.

Where you'll work

Food and beverage manufacturing plants, agricultural processing facilities, and Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) conglomerates.

🧵
👕 Manufacturing

Textile Engineering

What you'll do

Engineer fibers, yarns, and fabrics. Develop new textile machinery processes, sustainable fabrics, and quality control systems for garment production.

Where you'll work

Garment manufacturing hubs, synthetic fiber production plants, and textile quality assurance laboratories.

⛏️
⛰️ Resource

Mining Engineering

What you'll do

Plan and execute the safe, efficient extraction of minerals and resources from the earth using underground and open-pit mining techniques.

Where you'll work

Mining operations, resource exploration companies, geological testing firms, and explosive engineering agencies.

🛢️
⛰️ Resource

Petroleum Engineering

What you'll do

Design methods for extracting oil and natural gas from reservoirs deep underground, optimizing drilling equipment and recovery processes.

Where you'll work

Energy corporations, offshore drilling rigs, petroleum refineries, and pipeline management firms.

🔬
🧪 Research

Metallurgical Engineering & Materials Science

What you'll do

Study the physical and chemical behavior of metallic elements, develop new alloys, and test composites for structural or electronic applications.

Where you'll work

Steel and metal refineries, manufacturing R&D labs, semiconductor firms, and aerospace developers.

🏛️
🎨 Creative Design

Architecture

What you'll do

Blend art and engineering to design functional, safe, and aesthetically pleasing buildings, focusing on spatial planning and environmental impact.

Where you'll work

Architectural design studios, urban planning firms, real estate development companies, and independent practices.

🚢
Design & Hydrodynamics

Naval Architecture

What you'll do

Design the hull form, structure, and internal layout of ships and marine vessels, ensuring stability, buoyancy, and hydrodynamic efficiency.

Where you'll work

Shipyards, maritime design consultancies, naval defense contractors, and offshore engineering firms.

⚙️
Systems Operation

Marine Engineering

What you'll do

Oversee the design, installation, and maintenance of a vessel's internal operational systems, including steering, ventilation, and power generation.

Where you'll work

Commercial shipping companies, cruise lines, maritime regulatory agencies, and vessel maintenance facilities.

🏗️
Infrastructure

Port and Harbour Engineering

What you'll do

Design and construct coastal infrastructure such as breakwaters, jetties, cargo terminals, and dry docks to facilitate safe maritime transport and logistics.

Where you'll work

Port authorities, coastal construction firms, global logistics hubs, and heavy civil engineering consultancies.

🌊
Environmental

River and Coastal Engineering

What you'll do

Manage coastal erosion, flood defenses, and river navigability by designing sustainable marine structures and executing comprehensive dredging plans.

Where you'll work

Environmental agencies, government water resource departments, dredging companies, and marine surveying firms.

🔌
Control Systems

Marine Electrical & Electronics Engineering

What you'll do

Develop and maintain complex marine power distribution networks, automated control systems, and highly calibrated onboard navigation equipment.

Where you'll work

Marine technology manufacturers, ship repair yards, offshore wind farms, and maritime electronics suppliers.

🔧
Machinery

Marine Mechanical Engineering

What you'll do

Focus on the mechanical components of marine vessels, maintaining and optimizing heavy machinery, main engines, compressors, and hydraulic systems.

Where you'll work

Engine manufacturing plants, commercial fleet maintenance crews, offshore oil and gas rigs, and maritime defense sectors.

🧭
Navigation & Command

Nautical Science

What you'll do

Master ship navigation, meteorology, cargo handling, and maritime law to safely operate and command commercial vessels across international waters.

Where you'll work

Merchant navy fleets, international shipping corporations, maritime training academies, and harbor pilotage services.

🚀
Aerospace Dynamics

Propulsion and Flight Vehicles

What you'll do

Design, test, and optimize aerodynamic structures and propulsion engines (jet, turboprop, and rocket) to achieve maximum thrust and fuel efficiency.

Where you'll work

Aerospace manufacturing companies, defense contractors, aviation research laboratories, and national space agencies.

✈️
Aviation Electronics

Avionics

What you'll do

Engineer the electronic systems used in aircraft and spacecraft, including communications, radar, flight control algorithms, and collision avoidance systems.

Where you'll work

Aircraft production facilities, airline maintenance hubs, defense technology firms, and UAV (drone) manufacturing startups.

🛰️
Flight Systems

Electrical Systems & Instrumentation

What you'll do

Design the power generation architectures, complex wiring networks, and highly calibrated sensors required to monitor and control aerospace vehicle performance.

Where you'll work

Aerospace integration facilities, satellite development labs, aviation testing centers, and military technology organizations.

🛢️
Chemical Engineering

Fuel and Propellant Engineering

What you'll do

Formulate and test advanced solid and liquid propellants, managing combustion stability, energy density, and the safe storage of volatile materials.

Where you'll work

Propulsion test facilities, specialized chemical manufacturing plants, space exploration agencies, and missile defense programs.

🌌
Orbital Tech

Space Systems Engineering

What you'll do

Integrate complex subsystems for satellites, planetary probes, and spacecraft, ensuring survivability and mission success in the extreme environment of outer space.

Where you'll work

Satellite communications companies, national space programs, commercial spaceflight corporations, and orbital research institutes.
