Career Opportunities

📚
Teaching

Subject Teacher or Tutor

Description

Teach a subject such as Myanmar, English, mathematics, physics, or chemistry. School teaching may require an education qualification or other current teaching credentials.

Key Skills

Subject knowledge, clear explanation, lesson preparation, patience.

🗂️
Entry-level

Research or Project Assistant

Description

Support a university, research team, or community project by gathering information, organizing records, and preparing summaries under supervision.

Key Skills

Reading, note-taking, careful record keeping, basic research and writing.

📈
Quantitative

Junior Data or Records Assistant

Description

Help offices or organizations enter, check, and summarize information. Mathematics, statistics, or computer science study and spreadsheet skills are especially useful.

Key Skills

Accuracy, spreadsheets, basic statistics, data privacy.

🏢
Public service

Administrative Assistant

Description

Support day-to-day work in public offices, schools, businesses, or civil society organizations through document handling and coordination. Public-service roles may require competitive examinations.

Key Skills

Written communication, organization, office software, public service.
