Defense & Military

Commit to service, leadership, and national security through specialized training in military tactics, engineering, and strategic studies.

Academies for this Field

Defence Services Academy (DSA) Defence Services Technological Academy (DSTA) Defence Services Medical Academy (DSMA) DSINPS

Career Opportunities

⚔️
Leadership

Commissioned Officer

Description

Lead and command military personnel, oversee tactical operations, and ensure the readiness and discipline of armed forces units.

Key Skills

Strategic leadership, crisis management, tactical planning, physical endurance.

⚙️
Technical

Military Engineer

Description

Design, build, and maintain military infrastructure, combat vehicles, defense systems, and communication networks.

Key Skills

Civil/mechanical engineering, project management, logistics, defense technology.

⚕️
Healthcare

Military Medical Officer

Description

Provide medical care to service members, manage field hospitals, and oversee health and sanitation during deployments.

Key Skills

Trauma care, emergency medicine, military logistics, public health.

📡
Strategic

Intelligence Analyst

Description

Gather, analyze, and interpret information regarding security threats to provide actionable intelligence for military commanders.

Key Skills

Data analysis, cybersecurity, risk assessment, critical thinking.
