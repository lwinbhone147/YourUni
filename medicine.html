Explore Career Paths

🩺
🔥 High Demand

General Practitioner (GP)

What you'll do

Diagnose illnesses, prescribe treatments, manage chronic disorders, and provide primary healthcare services to patients of all ages.

Where you'll work

General hospitals (Yangon General, Mandalay General), private clinics, community health care centers, and wellness facilities.

😷
🏆 Specialized

General Surgeon

What you'll do

Perform operative procedures to treat injuries, diseases, and deformities, managing pre-operative and post-operative patient care.

Where you'll work

Public surgical wards, specialized emergency hospitals, trauma centers, and private surgical centers.

👶
🍼 Care

Pediatrician

What you'll do

Provide medical care, vaccinations, physical growth monitoring, and disease treatment specifically for infants, children, and adolescents.

Where you'll work

Children's hospitals (Yankin Children's Hospital), maternity wards, pediatric clinics, and child welfare institutions.

💉

Anesthesiologist

What you'll do

Administer pain-relief medications and anesthetics during surgical procedures while continuously monitoring patient vital signs.

Where you'll work

Surgical operating theaters, intensive care units (ICU), emergency rooms, and pain management clinics.

🫀
📈 Fast Growing

Cardiologist

What you'll do

Diagnose and treat diseases of the cardiovascular system, including heart failure, coronary artery disease, and heart rhythm disorders.

Where you'll work

Specialized cardiac centers, heart institutes, referral hospitals, and cardiovascular clinical research units.

🦷

Dental Surgeon / Dentist

What you'll do

Examine, diagnose, and treat oral health conditions, perform dental surgeries, correct alignments, and educate patients on oral hygiene.

Where you'll work

Dental hospitals, private dental practices, cosmetic dentistry centers, and public oral health divisions.

💊
🔥 High Demand

Pharmacist

What you'll do

Dispense medications, guide patients on drug dosages and side effects, and oversee drug manufacturing quality control standards.

Where you'll work

Hospital pharmacies, retail pharmaceutical chains, drug manufacturing companies, and regulatory health boards.

🩻

Radiologist

What you'll do

Interpret medical diagnostic images such as X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, and ultrasounds to assist doctors in diagnosing conditions.

Where you'll work

Diagnostic imaging centers, hospital radiology departments, oncology units, and teleradiology providers.

🔬
🧪 Lab Tech

Medical Laboratory Scientist

What you'll do

Analyze blood, tissue, and bodily fluid samples using laboratory equipment to detect pathogens, genetic markers, and chemical levels.

Where you'll work

Pathology labs, blood banks, public health testing facilities, and medical research institutes.

🩺
🔥 High Demand

Registered Nurse (RN)

What you'll do

Administer direct patient care, give medications, track patient recovery progress, and support doctors during clinical procedures.

Where you'll work

Public hospitals, outpatient clinics, nursing homes, rehabilitation facilities, and private patient care agencies.

🤰

Obstetrician & Gynecologist

What you'll do

Provide medical care related to female reproductive health, manage pregnancy and childbirth, and perform gynecological surgeries.

Where you'll work

Maternity hospitals, women's health clinics, reproductive health centers, and general surgical hospitals.

🌐
💼 Policy

Public Health Specialist

What you'll do

Develop community health initiatives, track epidemic patterns, manage disease prevention campaigns, and advise on health policy.

Where you'll work

World Health Organization (WHO), UNICEF, Ministry of Health divisions, non-governmental organizations (NGOs), and global health bodies.

🏋️‍♂️

Physiotherapist

What you'll do

Help patients recover physical movement, rebuild strength, and reduce pain following surgery, injury, or neurological illness.

Where you'll work

Rehabilitation clinics, sports medicine facilities, orthopedic hospitals, and private physical therapy practices.

🧪
🔬 Research

Clinical Research Scientist

What you'll do

Conduct clinical trials to test new pharmaceutical drugs, medical devices, and treatment protocols for safety and efficacy.

Where you'll work

Pharmaceutical R&D laboratories, medical universities, contract research organizations (CROs), and biotech institutes.

⚙️

Biomedical Engineer

What you'll do

Design, build, and maintain sophisticated medical equipment such as prosthetics, artificial organs, imaging machines, and patient monitors.

Where you'll work

Medical equipment manufacturers, hospital engineering divisions, biotech firms, and healthcare hardware suppliers.
