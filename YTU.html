Yangon Technological University (YTU)

Yangon Technological University is one of the premier engineering universities in Myanmar, offering high-quality education and research opportunities in engineering and architecture.

Official Site:

ytu.edu.mm

📍 Campus Location: [Insein Township, Yangon]

Offered Degrees (ဘွဲ့များ)

  • Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) 5 Years
  • Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.) 5 Years
  • Master of Engineering (M.E.) 2 Years
  • Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) 3+ Years
