Myanmar Aerospace Engineering University (MAEU)

Specialized engineering institution focusing on aerospace, avionics, space technology, and advanced mechanical engineering.

Official Site:

maeu.edu.mm

📍 Campus Location: [Meiktila, Mandalay]

Offered Degrees (ဘွဲ့များ)

  • Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) - အင်ဂျင်နီယာဘွဲ့6 Years
  • Master of Engineering (M.E.) - အင်ဂျင်နီယာမဟာဘွဲ့2 Years

Majors / Specialties (အထူးပြုဘာသာရပ်များ)

Propulsion and Flight Vehicles Avionics Electrical Systems and Instrumentation Fuel and Propellant Space Systems Engineering
