Description

Oversee forest conservation, manage timber resources sustainably, and protect wildlife habitats from illegal logging or degradation.

Key Skills

Forest management, ecology, GIS mapping, environmental law enforcement.

Description

Advise organizations on environmental impact, sustainability practices, and compliance with national ecological regulations.

Key Skills

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA), sustainability planning, data analysis.
