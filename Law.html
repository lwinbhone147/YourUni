Career Opportunities

📄
Entry-level

Legal Assistant

Description

Support a lawyer or legal office with document preparation, case-file organization, legal research, and client administration. This role does not itself authorize someone to practice as a lawyer.

Key Skills

Careful reading, legal research, writing, confidentiality, organization.

🏛️
Public service

Court or Legal Documentation Clerk

Description

Assist with filing, records, notices, and routine documentation in a court, law office, or public institution. Hiring rules and required qualifications vary by employer.

Key Skills

Accuracy, document handling, clear writing, professional conduct.

🔎
Business support

Compliance or Contract Assistant

Description

Help an organization maintain records, review routine documents, and track compliance tasks under the supervision of qualified legal or compliance staff.

Key Skills

Reading contracts, record keeping, research, attention to detail.

📝
Further pathway

Public Service or Legal Research

Description

A law degree can support applications to public-service, policy, or research roles. Government appointments and senior advisory positions may require examinations, additional study, or substantial experience.

Key Skills

Legal reading, evidence-based writing, analysis, public-service knowledge.
