Bago University

Bago University is a public university, located in Bago, Myanmar that offers bachelor's and post-graduate degree programs in Arts and Science.

Official Site:

bagouni.moe.edu.mm

📍 Campus Location: [ Oakthar Myothit, Bago]

Offered Degrees (ဘွဲ့များ)

  • Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) - ဝိဇ္ဇာဘွဲ့4 Years
  • Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) - သိပ္ပံဘွဲ့4 Years
  • Bachelor of Law (LL.B.) - ဥပဒေဘွဲ့5 Years

Majors / Specialties (အထူးပြုဘာသာရပ်များ)
